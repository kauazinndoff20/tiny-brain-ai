Tiny Brain AI

An independent Artificial Intelligence research and development project.

About the Project

Tiny Brain AI is an experimental artificial intelligence project focused on understanding, developing, training, and improving independent neural network models.

The project aims to explore machine learning, natural language processing, and multilingual artificial intelligence.

Main Objectives

- Develop neural networks from the ground up.
- Study and experiment with Transformer architectures.
- Explore multilingual language processing.
- Build and train original AI models.
- Develop an accessible conversational AI system.
